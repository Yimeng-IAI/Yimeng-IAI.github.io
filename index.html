Biography

I am a postdoctoral researcher at the Institute of AI for Industries, Chinese Academy of Sciences. I received my Ph.D. in Control Theory and Engineering from Southeast University in 2026 and visited Loughborough University from 2023 to 2025.

My research focuses on disturbance rejection control, model predictive control, and reinforcement learning, with applications to motor drives and robotic systems.

  • Finite-control-set MPC
  • Disturbance rejection
  • Reinforcement learning
  • Motor drives and robotics

Honors

March 2024 · Bristol, UK

IEEE ICIT 2024 Best Theory Paper Prize

Our paper on finite-control-set control for permanent magnet synchronous motor drives received the Best Theory Paper Prize. The method uses a control Lyapunov function to establish closed-loop stability under disturbances.
