Biography

I am a postdoctoral researcher at the Institute of AI for Industries, Chinese Academy of Sciences.

I earned my B.E. in Automation in 2019 from the School of Automation at Southeast University, where I continued into a combined master's and doctoral program and received my Ph.D. in Control Theory and Engineering in 2026. Prof. Jun Yang and Prof. Shihua Li co-supervised me during both stages. From 2023 to 2025, I was a CSC-funded visiting doctoral researcher at Loughborough University under Prof. Wen-Hua Chen.

My research focuses on disturbance rejection control, model predictive control, and reinforcement learning, with applications to motor drives and robotic systems.

  • Finite-control-set MPC
  • Disturbance rejection
  • Reinforcement learning
  • Motor drives and robotics

Honors

March 2024 · Bristol, UK

IEEE ICIT 2024 Best Theory Paper Prize

Our paper on finite-control-set control for permanent magnet synchronous motor drives received the Best Theory Paper Prize. The method uses a control Lyapunov function to establish closed-loop stability under disturbances.
